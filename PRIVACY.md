# Privacy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

**Crate does not collect, send or share any information about you.**

## The app

- There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
- Your songs, projects, exports and settings stay on your Mac: in `~/Music/Crate`, in the app's own folder at `~/Library/Application Support/Crate`, and in the app's preferences.
- The app makes no connections to the internet.
- While it is open, Crate listens on your own Mac (address 127.0.0.1, port 8790) so that an AI app you choose, such as Claude Code, can control it. It refuses connections from other computers.

## If you connect Claude

Connecting Claude is optional. If you do, what you type to Claude and what Claude reads from Crate about your song are handled by Anthropic under its own terms and privacy policy, not by Crate.

## The project page and the repository

The project page loads its fonts from Google Fonts and a 3D library from jsDelivr, and the page and the code are hosted by GitHub. Those services see the ordinary details of a web request, such as your IP address, as with any website. The page itself sets no cookies and runs no analytics.
